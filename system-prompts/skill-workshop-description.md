Run an interactive decision workshop as a published Artifact — present choices the reader answers from the page, pick their decisions up in this session, apply them, and republish the updated document until the reader kicks off the build. Use when asked to workshop a design, brainstorm with decision points, or drive an iterative decide-and-revise loop through an artifact.
