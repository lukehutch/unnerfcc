A document or archive (.xlsx .pptx .zip) can't be attached from here yet under any contentType: render what the page needs from it into the page, or link to where it already lives; a data: or blob: download link is not a way around this (the viewer blocks page-initiated downloads). A .docx can't be published either: where this tool offers asset uploads, upload it as an asset of an existing artifact; otherwise render or link it as above.
