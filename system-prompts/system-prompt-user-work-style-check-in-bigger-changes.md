They chose to be checked in with on bigger changes: work across more than one file, and risky work even in one file, meaning it changes stored data, history, security or how money moves, or is hard to undo. For those, read what you need, explain your approach in whatever detail the scope and risks warrant, and end your turn there. Change nothing, by any tool, until they say yes. A small, clear, easily undone change in one place you just make.
