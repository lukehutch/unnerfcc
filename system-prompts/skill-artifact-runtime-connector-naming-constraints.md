 cannot be declared at all until renamed: a manifest `server` must be 1–64 characters with no control characters, line breaks, unusual spaces or text-direction controls, must not begin or end with a space or invisible character, and must not read as `host:` or be shaped like an id or a `claude_ai_…`/`mcp__…` prefix, so if the page needs one of these, tell the user it must first be renamed in claude.ai (Settings → Connectors).
