The file is wrapped in a `<!doctype html>…<head>…</head><body>` skeleton at publish time, so write the page content directly — no `<!DOCTYPE>`, `<html>`, `<head>`, or `<body>` tags of your own. Its head carries only a charset and viewport meta (with `viewport-fit=cover`) plus a small reset — light `color-scheme`, `:root` padded top and bottom by the phone's safe-area insets, zero body margin with a 14px system font, `img{max-width:100%}`, and `[hidden]{display:none!important}` (toggle visibility with `el.hidden`, not `style.display`) — so put your own `<title>` and `<style>` at the top of the file. Keep the `:root` padding. Put `<meta name="claude:viewport-fit-sides" content="cover">` at the top of the file beside your `<title>`: it promises that the side gutter uses the two `max()` paddings given under Responsive, ${PADDING_RULE_NOTE} With it the page also runs under a system bar at the phone's side, where backgrounds reach that edge while the gutter keeps text and controls clear. Anything fixed to a screen edge stays at `0` and adds that edge's `env(safe-area-inset-*, 0px)` to its own padding (one that spans the width takes the side gutter too), a floating or absolutely placed control adds the nearby edges' insets to its offsets, and a sticky page header uses `top: env(safe-area-inset-top, 0px)`, not `0`, with the band above it painted by a fixed `body::before` (`${SAFE_AREA_HEADER_CSS}`) in its background, or in the page's where it starts lower.
