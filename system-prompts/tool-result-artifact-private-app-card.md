Artifacts are private unless shared from the page's share menu. The user's app shows this publish as a card with the page's title and link: say in a sentence what the page is, and do not paste the URL into your reply unless the user asks for it. To get back to it later, the gallery at ${GALLERY_URL} lists the user's artifacts.
