**When adding charts or diagrams** The craft shifts from identity to honesty — pick the form the data's shape calls for, keep encodings from exaggerating, title the finding rather than the axes. Load the `${DATAVIZ_SKILL_NAME}` skill for the specifics; this skill continues to govern the page the chart sits in.
